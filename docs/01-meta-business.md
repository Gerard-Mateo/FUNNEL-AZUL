# Step 1 – Meta Business setup

Everything (Page, Instagram, ad account, WhatsApp) must live inside **one Business Portfolio**.

1. **Facebook Page** for the brand (facebook.com/pages/create). Name, logo, cover photo, a few posts.
2. **Instagram professional account** (Business). Post 6–9 nice photos/reels first so it doesn't look empty — people will check the profile before writing.
3. **Business Portfolio**: go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) → Create portfolio, with your real name and business email.
4. Inside the portfolio → **Settings** (business.facebook.com/settings):
   - *Accounts → Pages* → add your Page.
   - *Accounts → Instagram accounts* → connect Instagram.
   - *Accounts → Ad accounts* → create one. **Currency: USD, time zone: America/Guayaquil** (can't be changed later).
   - *Billing & payments* → add a card (debit/credit cards with international purchases enabled work).
5. **Business verification** (Settings → Security Center): upload your RUC. Not required to start, but it raises your WhatsApp limits and protects your ad account. Do it in the first weeks.
6. **Link WhatsApp to your Page** (Page settings → Linked accounts → WhatsApp) — you'll do this with the bot's number in step 4 (or with the WhatsApp Business app number if you start with Plan B).

✅ Done when: Page + Instagram + ad account (USD) appear under the same portfolio, and the card is added.
