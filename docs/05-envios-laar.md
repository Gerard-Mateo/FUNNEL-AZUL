# Step 3 – Delivery with LAAR Courier

## Open your account

1. Contact LAAR Courier ([laarcourier.com](https://laarcourier.com)) and ask for a **business / corporate client account** (cuenta empresarial). Ask them:
   - Rate for a small package (~0.5 kg, a notebook) to: **your own city**, **main cities** (Quito, Guayaquil, Cuenca), **rest of the country**, and **Galápagos** (if you'll ship there).
   - Do they **pick up at your store** (retiro a domicilio) and from what number of packages?
   - How do you create guías (web portal? Excel upload?) and how do customers track them.
   - Do they offer **cash on delivery (contra entrega / recaudo)**? Could be a 3rd payment option later.
2. Put the rate in `SHIPPING_COST`. If rates differ a lot by city, tell me and I'll make the bot charge by city/zone.

## What the bot already collects for each guía

Name · cédula · WhatsApp phone · city · full address with reference. You'll find it in the alert you receive and in `data/orders.json`.

## Packaging (part of the experience!)

- Rigid envelope or small box so the corners don't bend.
- A thank-you card + sticker; ask them to tag you on Instagram → free content for ads.
